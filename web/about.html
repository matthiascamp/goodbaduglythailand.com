The Good, Bad & Ugly of Thailand is built around Merv’s first-hand perspective. He helps visitors plan better trips, people considering a move understand daily life, and those staying longer prepare extension application paperwork.

Living somewhere for years gives you context. It does not make every rule simple or every answer permanent. Immigration requirements change, and official decisions belong to Thai authorities. Merv’s paperwork service is administrative help from lived experience, not licensed immigration or legal advice.
