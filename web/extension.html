Before you ask

Good questions.

Does the service include the immigration fee?

Merv’s paperwork preparation fee appears after you select your services and review your request. Government fees and other third-party charges are separate.

Will Merv submit the application for me?

No. Merv prepares the paperwork for you to review. You remain responsible for signing and submitting it to the relevant Thai immigration office.

Will the extension be approved?

Thai immigration makes that decision. Merv cannot guarantee an outcome and will confirm whether your request fits his paperwork service before you commit.

What if I am not sure which extension I need?

Say so in your enquiry. Merv can confirm whether he can help with the paperwork you need, and you should verify current eligibility and requirements with Thai immigration or a licensed adviser.
